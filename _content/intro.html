I am a postdoctoral researcher at Imperial College London, working with Stefanos Zafeiriou and Rıza Alp Güler. I work on the capture and synthesis of realistic virtual humans: building photorealistic, animatable avatars from video and multi-view capture, and making them move and interact believably inside 3D scenes.

I obtained my Ph.D. from the University of Tübingen and the Max Planck Institute for Informatics, advised by Gerard Pons-Moll. I have also been a visiting student at KAUST with Peter Wonka, and interned at Snap Research in New York and Huawei Noah's Ark Lab in London. Before my Ph.D., I received a Master's in Computer Science from Saarland University. My work was shortlisted for the Best Paper Award at CVPR 2021.

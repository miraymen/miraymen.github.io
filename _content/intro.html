I am a postdoctoral researcher at Imperial College London, advised by Stefanos Zafeiriou and Rıza Alp Güler. My research is on the capture and synthesis of realistic virtual humans: building photorealistic, animatable avatars from video and multi-view capture, including with AI agents that plan the reconstruction, and making them move and interact believably inside 3D scenes. My work was shortlisted for the Best Paper Award at CVPR 2021.

I obtained my Ph.D. from the University of Tübingen and the Max Planck Institute for Informatics, supervised by Gerard Pons-Moll. I have also been a visiting student at KAUST with Peter Wonka, and interned at Snap Research in New York and Huawei Noah's Ark Lab in London. Before my Ph.D., I received a Master's in Computer Science from Saarland University.

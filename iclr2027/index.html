    How it's computed

    Average: the mean of the paper's ratings. Confidence-weighted average: each rating counts in proportion to its reviewer's confidence,

    Σ (rating × confidence) / Σ confidence

    For example, ratings 3, 3 and 2 with confidences 3, 3 and 2 give (9 + 9 + 4) / 8 = 2.75, against a plain average of 2.67: the reject counts for 2/8 of the weight instead of a third. When all reviewers are equally confident, the two averages are the same.

    Top X % counts the papers tied with this one as level with it: (papers above + papers tied + 1) / all papers. Ties broken by confidence ranks by the average first and uses the weighted average only among papers with the same average.

    Who gave the lowest score compares the confidence of the reviewer(s) with the lowest rating with the confidence of the others.

    Area chairs decide from the reviews and the discussion, not from an average; this page only places the scores. Scores change while reviewers edit their reviews: they are as of the download time above.
